# Visual minimalism policy

Status: canonical companion to `GPT_APP_BOARD_FIRST_V2`.

## Lowest-sufficient rule

Background and non-character assets are omitted by default. A slide declares the
lowest level that still carries its story:

- `NONE`: no location drawing is needed;
- `SYMBOLIC`: one or two cues establish context;
- `LOCATION_ANCHOR`: a few spatial anchors are needed for action/continuity;
- `FULL_SCENE`: environment geometry itself carries story information.

`FULL_SCENE` requires an explicit story reason. Empty space is valid composition.

## Asset budget

Only story-bearing or contact-bearing objects are mandatory. Decorative furniture,
plants, wall art, lamps, shelves, appliances, props, packaging, and texture are
omitted unless they establish place, enable an action, carry continuity, or support
the punchline.

This is not a blank-background quota. The operator chooses the lowest sufficient
level per beat.

## QC

Extra environment detail is a failure when it competes with the beat, increases
continuity burden without adding meaning, or pushes the result toward generic
polished AI/webtoon scenery.
