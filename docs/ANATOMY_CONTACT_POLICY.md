# Anatomy/contact guard

Status: canonical supplement for GPT_APP_BOARD_FIRST_V1.

Use this guard only for shots with elevated manual-action ambiguity.

Apply when a character simultaneously handles multiple objects, combines a prop
contact with self-touch, shares a story-bearing prop with another character, or
uses crossed/occluded arms whose ownership may become unclear.

When triggered, declare semantic limb roles, required contacts, forbidden extra
or disconnected limbs, and a simplification fallback. Do not impose a universal
two-visible-hands rule and do not impose a global fixed action-count cap.

Story-bearing prop/device contact has priority over decorative gesture. If both
cannot coexist cleanly, simplify the decorative gesture or restage the beat at an
equivalent lower-contact moment.

QC rejects duplicated or anatomically unrooted limbs and ambiguous ownership of
declared contacts. Anatomically valid occlusion is allowed.
